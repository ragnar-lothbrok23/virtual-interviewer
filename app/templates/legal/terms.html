Terms of Use

Version {{ version }}

1. Using the platform

The Virtual Interviewer platform lets candidates apply for roles and take AI-assisted interviews, and lets the hiring organisation's staff review applications. You must provide accurate information and keep your password secret.

2. Honest participation
  • Answer interview questions yourself, without help from other people or tools, unless the interview says otherwise.
  • Do not attempt to manipulate the AI evaluation (for example by giving it instructions inside your answers or resume).
  • Do not share interview questions or access links with others.

Integrity signals (such as leaving the interview window or another person appearing on camera) are recorded and shown to recruiters.

3. Accounts

Administrators may deactivate accounts that break these terms or are used to misuse the platform. You can delete your own account at any time.

4. AI evaluation

Scores are produced automatically and may contain errors. They help recruiters but do not by themselves decide the outcome of an application.

5. Availability

We aim to keep the platform available but cannot guarantee uninterrupted service. If an interview is interrupted by a technical problem, you will be able to resume or reschedule it.

6. Privacy

How we handle your personal data is described in the Privacy Policy.
