Privacy Policy

Version {{ version }}

This policy explains what personal data the Virtual Interviewer platform collects, why, who can see it, how long it is kept, and the choices you have. By creating an account you agree to this processing.

1. Data we collect
  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored only as a secure hash), role, and sign-in history.
  • Resume: the file you upload, the text extracted from it, and the structured profile produced from it (skills, experience, education, projects, certifications).
  • Interview data: video and audio recorded during an interview, transcripts of your answers, answer scores, speaking pace and filler words, facial expression and attentiveness estimates, and integrity signals (for example, whether a face was visible or another person appeared on camera).
  • Technical data: IP address and timestamps of security events (sign-ins, password changes), kept in an audit log to protect accounts.
2. Why we use it
  • To match your profile to job roles and calculate a match score.
  • To run interviews, ask follow-up questions, and produce a scorecard for recruiters.
  • To schedule interviews and send you notifications.
  • To keep accounts secure and investigate misuse.

Automated scores support recruiters; they are not the sole basis of a hiring decision. Recruiters review the scorecard, transcripts and evidence and make the final decision.

3. AI processing and service providers

Resume text and interview transcripts or audio are sent to Google's Gemini API for analysis (parsing, scoring, transcription and semantic matching). Facial emotion analysis runs on our own servers. Email notifications are sent through Gmail. These providers process data on our behalf only to deliver these functions.

4. Who can see your data

Recruiters (HR users) and administrators of the hiring organisation can see your profile, applications, scores, transcripts and evidence. Other candidates can never see your data.

5. How long we keep it

Your data is kept while your account exists. Interview recordings may be deleted earlier according to the organisation's retention schedule. When you delete your account, your profile, applications, uploaded files and analysis results are permanently deleted. A minimal record of security events (with your email address) is kept in the audit log for security and legal purposes.

6. Your rights
  • Access and portability: download all your data from Account settings → Download my data.
  • Correction: update your name and email in Account settings, or upload an updated resume.
  • Deletion: permanently delete your account from Account settings → Delete account.
  • Withdraw consent: delete your account at any time; processing stops immediately.
7. Security

Connections are encrypted (HTTPS), passwords are hashed, accounts are protected against repeated guessing, and access is restricted by role. Security events are logged and reviewed.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, you will be asked to review and accept the new version the next time you sign in.

9. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact the administrator of the organisation that invited you to apply.
